# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind + GSAP, as specified in the user's brief. Deploy target undecided by the user ("ainda não sei"), so the site is built as a static export (`output: 'export'`) that works on Vercel and on ordinary shared hosting (FTP upload of `out/`).

## Users

Business owners and managers in Maceió/Alagoas who need visual communication for their business. Confirmed by the user: both local commerce (street shops, bars, restaurants, clinics, schools) and brand/chain stores in shopping malls hire Ponto X in equal measure. They arrive wanting to see real work and get a quote quickly, usually via WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Landing page for PONTO X Comunicação Visual. It shows what the company makes (facades, signs, banners, vehicle wraps, adhesives, acrylics, displays, printed materials), proves it with real photos and videos of client work, and turns visits into WhatsApp quote requests. Success = a visitor taps "Solicitar orçamento" or "Quero um projeto como este".

## Positioning

Active in Maceió since 2007. One supplier for the whole visual identity of a physical business, from the facade and sign outside to the counter materials inside. The hero video dramatizes this: a plain white store builds its full identity piece by piece.

## Operating Context

Visitors mostly come from Instagram (@pontox_maceio) and Google, often on phones. The conversion channel is WhatsApp (82) 99111-8530. Address: Av. Dr. Antônio Gomes de Barros, 930 – Centro Comercial Wilson Cavalcante – Jatiúca, Maceió – AL. Hours: Monday–Friday 08h–18h, closed weekends.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services are limited to exactly: Fachadas, Letreiros; Lonas convencionais e lonas em estrutura, Wind banner; Plotagem de veículos e paredes, Adesivos personalizados; Acrílicos, Displays; Crachás, Cartões de visita, Panfletos. No other services may be listed.
- The work process ("Envie sua ideia → Receba a proposta → Produção e instalação") is unconfirmed by the company and stays marked [CONFIRMAR].
- All site copy is in Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR).

## Brand Commitments

- Logo file: `Logotipo Ponto X em Vermelho e Branco.png` (white "PONTO", small "COMUNICAÇÃO VISUAL", large red X with sharp diagonal cuts, on black). Always used as the image, never redrawn.
- Slogan: "Sua marca em evidência desde 2007."
- Voice: serious, confident, professional. Humor appears only in the Videos section title ("Aqui o letreiro acende, e o cliente também.").
- The company's own Instagram art uses red diagonal bands on white and black.

## Evidence on Hand

Client logos in the brand strip are cropped only from Ponto X's own project photos/video frames (owner request, 2026-11-15), never redrawn or downloaded; clients without a clean mark appear as text. Le Frutan in IMG_6551 confirmed by the user via the identical logo in `lefrutan.jpeg`.
- Absent and must never be fabricated: testimonials, ratings, client counts, numbers, partner names, certifications, guarantees, project years, sizes, materials, deadlines.

## Product Principles

1. Real work is the proof: show actual photos and videos, never invented claims.
2. Every path ends in a WhatsApp conversation with a pre-filled message.
3. The store is the protagonist: UI stays minimal around imagery.
4. Unknown facts stay visibly marked ([NOME DO CLIENTE], [SEGMENTO], [CONFIRMAR]) until the company confirms them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus, pt-BR alt text for every image, prefers-reduced-motion respected (scroll video still usable, LED/glow effects disabled).
