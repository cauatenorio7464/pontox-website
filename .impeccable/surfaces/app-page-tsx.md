## Scope

Landing page (app/page.tsx), Persuade mode. Audience: business owners in Maceió (local commerce and mall brands equally). Action: WhatsApp quote request. Proof: real client photos and videos only. Constraints: pt-BR copy, only the listed services, no invented facts, humor only in the Videos title. Build path: code-led (no image generation available).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page performs the company's own job. A blank white store builds the PONTO X identity as the visitor scrolls, then the page continues as a two-colour print job in black, red and white. It refuses the agency template of hero + icon cards + testimonials.

OWN-WORLD (owner palette update 2026-11-16): blue-black ground #12141C / #1A1D27, wine→crimson reds #5A0B17 → #B3152C (#E0324A only at glow tips), white text; the PONTO X logo keeps its own colours. The X's diagonal cut stays the lead motif: red bands at the X's angle, clipped corners on media (now with 16px rounding on the other corners), diagonal wipes. A diagonal glow sits strong only behind the closing CTA. Frosted glass on the floating header, project cards, process step cards, the CTA box, contact rows, secondary buttons and slider controls; never over the hero video. Big Shoulders Display for the hero H1/H2 only; Archivo everywhere else. Primary action: wine→crimson gradient button; secondary: glass.

STORY: The visitor watches the store transform, believes Ponto X can make every piece of it, checks real clients' work, and taps WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed paused first frame (white store at dusk). The H1 sits in Big Shoulders at ~7vw, bottom-left over the street, with a subtle bottom scrim, and lights up once like an LED. Logo, four links and "Orçamento" sit over a faint top scrim.

FORM: Pinned by the brief (scroll-scrubbed video hero), with taste-skill editorial-report section geometry. The concept-seed roll was skipped because the brief pinned the direction; no seed exists.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
